## Viega fonterra floorheating for Home Assistant

Home assistant Custom Component for reading data from Viega fonterra floorheating systems through modbus TCP. Implements smart control registers from //https://web-catalog.viega.com/de_AT/html/Montage/Flaechentemperierung/Fonterra/1a86c459c36246e4ac114094093e2e46_8_de_AT/pdf/ga_fonterra-smart-control_8_de_de.pdf

This custom component is experimental version with featured not (yet) present in standard Hass integration.
